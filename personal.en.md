# 💼 How I think about work

## Career direction

- I want a career where I can exercise product, project, people and technical management
  - So far my roles have leaned toward project and technical management; going forward I want to grow the product and people sides
- I want to build services that create entertainment experiences through video and audio — the kind of content people look forward to coming back to

## Choosing technology

- I think less about the architecture itself and more about what I want tests to guarantee, and which structure best supports that
- I choose architectures that are easy for the next person on the team to work with
- On the frontend I usually reach for designs built around Flux-style thinking
- Testability matters more to me than having everything perfectly uniform

## How I work

- I keep the scope of each change small enough that it can actually be verified
- I develop in small iterations to get feedback early
- I value collaboration and keep communication tight within the team

# 📱 Why I build mobile apps

- It is genuinely fun to see code I wrote or designed running in someone's hands
- Building apps that touch people's daily lives is what makes the work rewarding
